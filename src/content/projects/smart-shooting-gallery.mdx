## Overview & Introduction

The **Smart Glove-Based Laser Shooting Game** is a cyber-physical system combining advanced sensor technology, haptic interaction, and real-time audio-visual feedback. 

A wearable smart glove equipped with a thumb pressure sensor detects when the user presses against their middle finger, firing a focused red laser diode while activating an internal vibration motor for tactile feedback. The target system consists of a single dynamic target divided into three concentric clear acrylic rings, each embedded with **BPW34 silicon PIN photodiodes** to detect laser impact zones for tiered scoring.

Real-time feedback is provided through an RGB NeoPixel matrix, a 12V high-power LED strip driven by an IRF520 MOSFET, and an integrated sound system powered by a DFPlayer Mini and PAM8403 audio amplifier. Controlled via an **ESP32 microcontroller** and an **Arduino Mega** bridged wirelessly through a **NodeMCU (ESP8266)** module, the game features multiple modes (*Time Attack*) across Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels.

## Problem & Significance of Study

### 1. The Problem in Brief
In today's fast-paced environment, individuals and students face elevated stress levels and limited physical engagement. Traditional screen-based mobile and console games contribute to sedentary habits, eye strain, and overstimulation without encouraging physical interactivity.

### 2. Significance & Benefits
- **Active & Screen-Free Gameplay**: Encourages physical movement and hand-eye coordination without staring at mobile screens.
- **Immediate Stress Relief**: Combines physical target shooting precision with tactile haptic and audio-visual rewards.
- **Promotes Social Interaction**: Supports solo practice and competitive multiplayer leaderboards.

## Aim & Core Objectives

### Aim
Develop an interactive, smart glove-based laser target system that delivers an immersive physical gaming experience while promoting active engagement.

### Key Objectives
- **Precision Pressure Trigger**: Design a thumb-to-finger pressure trigger on the glove for laser firing paired with instantaneous haptic vibration confirmation.
- **Tiered 3-Ring Hit Detection**: Construct a 3-zone concentric acrylic target using BPW34 photodiodes and LM358 operational amplifiers to calculate precise scores based on impact location.
- **Wireless Inter-Module Bridge**: Implement wireless data transmission between the Arduino Mega (sensor & motor control) and ESP32 glove via a NodeMCU (ESP8266) module.
- **Scalable Difficulty & Movement**: Implement *Time Attack* game modes with dynamic target trajectories:
  - **Easy**: Linear X-axis movement along a pre-defined path.
  - **Medium**: 2D X and Y-axis pre-defined movement.
  - **Hard**: Randomized 2D X and Y-axis movement.
- **Multi-Modal Feedback**: Synchronize 14x8 NeoPixel matrix animations, 12V LED flashes, and DFPlayer Mini sound effects upon confirmed laser strikes.

## Proposed Solution & Features

1. **Pressure-Based Shooting Interaction**: Glove-embedded thumb pressure sensor triggers the red laser diode and vibration motor simultaneously.
2. **Concentric Ring Target Precision**: Three acrylic rings housing BPW34 photodiodes pass signals through LM358 op-amp conditioning to distinguish inner bullseye vs outer ring hits.
3. **Linear Actuator Motion System**: NEMA17 steppers driven by an A4988/CNC Shield assembly slide the target horizontally and vertically across precision rails.
4. **Wireless Telemetry**: ESP8266 NodeMCU bridges physical target event data to a Node.js WebSocket gateway and React web scoreboard.

## Hardware & System Component Breakdown

### 🔧 Linear Actuator System (Target Movement)
- **Arduino Mega**: Processes target positioning logic and limit switch feedback.
- **CNC Shield & A4988 Drivers**: Interfaces stepping sequences to NEMA17 motors.
- **NEMA17 Stepper Motors & Linear Rail**: Powers smooth X/Y target translation.
- **Limit Switches**: Prevents mechanical over-travel damage.

### 🎯 Target System (Hit Detection & Scoring)
- **BPW34 Photodiodes**: Detects Visible Red (650nm) laser pulses within microseconds.
- **LM358 Operational Amplifier IC**: Amplifies photodiode signals for high-precision digital logic conversion.
- **Layered Clear Acrylic Rings**: 3 distinct physical zones for tiered score calculation.

### ✋ Smart Glove (Player Interaction Unit)
- **ESP32 Microcontroller**: Samples pressure sensor inputs and controls laser/haptics via FreeRTOS tasks.
- **Red Laser Diode Module**: Controlled via 2N2222 NPN transistor switch with 1N4007 reverse-polarity protection.
- **3S Li-ion Battery & BMS 20A**: Portable power supply managed by an LM2596 buck converter and BMS protection board.

### 🔊 Visual & Audio Feedback System
- **DFPlayer Mini & PAM8403 Amplifier**: Plays real-time sound effects for hits, countdowns, and game over states.
- **NeoPixel 14x8 RGB LED Matrix**: Displays animated green "HIT" confirmations and score tallies.
- **12V Single-Color LED Strip & IRF520 MOSFET**: Delivers high-brightness flash feedback upon successful target impact.
