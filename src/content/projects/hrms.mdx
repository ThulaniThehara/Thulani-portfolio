## Overview

Enterprise **HRMS** is a cloud-native Human Resource Management System designed to streamline employee management, attendance, leave, and device allocation. It provides role-based experiences for Administrators, Managers, and Employees while integrating workplace services such as Google Calendar, Google Chat, Slack, and GitHub. Built with a serverless AWS architecture using AppSync, Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS, and Cognito, the system is designed for secure and scalable operations. An Amazon Bedrock-powered AI chatbot further enhances the platform by providing intelligent assistance for HR-related tasks.

## The Problem

Growing organizations face significant operational overhead when scaling HR processes:<br/>
1.Challenges due to fragmented HR and operational systems <br/>
2.Attendance, leave management, and productivity tracking operated independently <br/>
3.Manual attendance logging caused inaccuracies and delays <br/>
4.Leave approvals were time-consuming and lacked real-time visibility <br/>
5.No centralized system to track company devices and reported issues <br/>
6.Difficulty correlating developer work output (GitHub activity) with attendance records <br/>
7.Limited analytical insights for managers and administrators


## Goals

1.To design and develop a custom Human Resource Management System for Enlear<br/>
2.Automate HR workflows and reduce administrative overhead<br/>
3.Improve transparency, efficiency, and decision-making<br/>
4.Provide a scalable and secure platform tailored to Enlear’s workflow<br/>


## My Role & Key Decisions

1.Device management module for all 3 roles( including device assignment, device issue managment)<br/>
2.Integrated AWS subscription (graphQL) for internal system notifications with using AWS SQS.<br/>
3.Implemented browser session-based chat history for chatbot to retain conversations until the employee closes the tab or logs out. <br/>
4.Create test cases using vitest.<br/>

## What I Learned

- Designing serverless architectures demands thinking in asynchronous boundaries: delegating side-effects to queues like Amazon SQS keeps client interactions snappy and resilient.
- Strong GraphQL typing paired with Vitest unit tests provides unmatched confidence when modifying complex business calculation engines.
