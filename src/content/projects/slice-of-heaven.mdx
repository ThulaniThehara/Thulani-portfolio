## Overview

**Slice of Heaven** is a full-stack, editorial e-commerce platform designed for an artisanal cake shop and luxury patisserie. It provides a customer-facing shop for browsing, customizing, and ordering fresh celebration cakes, gateaux, muffins, and special promotional offers, alongside a comprehensive administrative dashboard for catalog management, promotional campaigns, and order dispatch tracking.

## Technology Stack & Architecture

The application is built using a decoupled MERN-style (MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js) architecture:

- **Frontend**: React 19, React Router DOM v7, Vite build system, Axios, `lucide-react` / `react-icons`, and custom Vanilla CSS with a centralized Design System.
- **Backend**: Node.js & Express.js (v5), MongoDB with Mongoose schemas, Multer & Cloudinary integration for image uploads, CORS middleware, and Dotenv configuration.
- **State Management**: React Context (`CartContext.jsx`) managing shopping bag items, quantity steppers, cart persistence, subtotal calculation, and dynamic top-bar free delivery threshold calculations.

## Design System & Aesthetics

The design follows a **Modern Bakery × Premium Editorial × Minimal Luxury** aesthetic:

## In-Depth Analysis of Every Page

### 1. Customer-Facing Pages

#### 🏡 Home Page (`/` or `/Home`)
- **Hero Slider**: Full-width auto-playing banner showcasing fresh artisanal signature cakes and promotional highlights.
- **Category Grid**: Interactive visual cards linking to targeted cake collections (*Birthday Cakes, Wedding Cakes, Chocolate Cakes, Signature Gateaux, Muffins, Cupcakes*).
- **Middle Story Bar**: Highlighting master pastry chef craftsmanship and fresh morning baking guarantees.
- **Main Offer Showcase**: Displays current active deals with discounted pricing cards.
- **Feature Grid**: Value propositions highlighting hygienic preparation, climate-controlled courier delivery, and custom cake inscriptions.

#### 🎂 Products Catalog Page (`/products` or `/categories/:type`)
- **Category Navigation Pills**: Scrollable filter bar to switch between categories (*All Delights, Birthday Cakes, Wedding Cakes, Chocolate Cakes, Signature Gateaux, Muffins, Cupcakes*).
- **In-Category Live Filter**: Real-time search bar to instantly filter cakes by flavor or name.
- **Dynamic Catalog Grid**: Displays product cards showing image, weight, category pill, price (Rs.), and quick action buttons.
- **State Handling**: Loading spinners, error fallback displays with retry buttons, and empty state illustrations when no items match search terms.

#### 🔍 Product Details Page (`/product/:slug`)
- **Visual Showcase**: High-res image frame with category pill overlay and trust badges (*Hygienic Prep, Safe Delivery, Fresh Ingredients*).
- **Product Metadata**: Displays price, weight in kg, status pill (*Freshly Baked to Order*), and flavor/recipe descriptions.
- **Interactive Stepper**: Allows customers to select quantities with live order total preview calculations.
- **Actions**:
  - **Add to Bag**: Pushes product into `CartContext` with feedback toast notification.
  - **Order Now**: Instantly bypasses cart and navigates directly to the checkout page with single-product order state.

#### 🏷️ Special Offers Page (`/offers` or `/Offers`)
- **Promotional Banner**: Dedicated landing page featuring discounted celebration gateaux.
- **Offer Cards**: Shows regular vs. discounted prices, calculated percentage savings badge, offer expiration dates, and direct order links for promotional deals.

#### 🛍️ Shopping Bag Page (`/cart`)
- **Free Delivery Progress Bar**: Interactive visual bar indicating progress toward unlocking FREE delivery (Rs. 5,000 threshold).
- **Item Management**: Quantity increase/decrease controls, single-item deletion, and clear bag option.
- **Order Summary Card**: Breakdown of items subtotal, estimated delivery fee (Rs. 250 or FREE), and total to pay, with a button to proceed to direct checkout.

#### 📋 Checkout & Direct Order Page (`/order`)
- **Custom Cake Message Inscription**: Allows customers to specify custom chocolate plaque messages (up to 50 characters) for each cake ordered.
- **Delivery Coordinates Form**: Collects customer Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Delivery Address, and requested Delivery Date.
- **Multi-Source Checkout Support**: Seamlessly processes orders originating from the cart, direct product "Order Now", or promotional offers.

#### 🎉 Order Confirmation Page (`/order-success`)
- **Confirmation Receipt**: Displays unique Order ID reference number (`#XXXXXX`).
- **Next Steps Timeline**: Guides customer through Email Dispatch &rarr; Fresh Artisanal Baking &rarr; Punctual Courier Delivery.

#### 📜 About Us & Contact Pages (`/aboutUs` & `/ContactUs`)
- Shares the story of the bakery, master pastry chefs, high-grade Belgian chocolate sourcing, store contact details, opening hours, bakery location, and feedback forms.

---

### 2. Admin Management Portal

#### 🖥️ Admin Dashboard (`/admin`)
- Central entry point for store managers, providing access to product inventory control, marketing campaigns, and fulfillment dispatch.

#### 📦 Product Inventory Management (`/ManageProducts`)
- **Product Form**: Allows admins to publish new cakes to the live store with fields for Product Name, Category dropdown, Price (Rs), Weight (kg), Description, Status (Active/Inactive), and Cloudinary image upload.

#### 🏷️ Promotional Offer Campaign Manager (`/ManageOffers`)
- **Offer Form**: Enables creation of limited-time deals with inputs for Regular Price, Discounted Price, automatically calculated percentage savings preview, Expiration Date picker, description, and promotional banner image upload.

#### 🚚 Order Dispatch & Kanban Fulfillment Board (`/ViewOrders`)
- **Kanban Board**: Organizes orders across 4 columns: *Pending*, *Processing*, *Completed*, and *Cancelled*.
- **Real-time Status Updating**: Dropdown controls on each card to change order status dynamically.
- **Order Details**: Displays order ID, customer contact info, delivery address, delivery date badge, items ordered breakdown, custom cake messages, and total price.

<Callout type="tip" title="Cart Context Persistence">
Utilizes React Context API (`CartContext`) with `localStorage` fallback to preserve shopping bag contents across page navigation and browser sessions.
