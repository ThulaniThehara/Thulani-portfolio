An employee asks the HRMS chatbot how many leave days they have left, then asks a follow-up a few minutes later — and the bot has forgotten everything. This article walks through solving that with browser-based `sessionStorage` rather than permanent database storage, keeping chat history available for the length of a session while clearing it automatically on logout.
